========================================================================
Linux AppImage
========================================================================

The Linux executable links SDL3, FFmpeg, OpenSSL, libstdc++ and libgcc statically. No shared copy of those libraries
is shipped. FFmpeg is the pinned LGPL-only build of tools/ffmpeg-bootstrap.sh (n7.1.1, commit
db69d06eeeab4f46da15030a80d539efb4503ca8); its licence text and source offer are FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt and
FFMPEG-SOURCE.txt. OpenSSL is the pinned 3.5.9 build of tools/openssl-bootstrap.sh; its licence text is
OpenSSL-LICENSE.txt. The Apache-2.0 text used by OpenSSL, Vulkan-Headers and Droid Sans is Apache-2.0.txt.

The Vulkan loader, the GPU drivers (Mesa RADV/ANV, NVIDIA), the X11/Wayland display servers and the audio servers are
provided by the player's system and are not part of the AppImage.

The port's own source code has no licence file yet; that choice belongs to the repository owner. See LEGAL.txt in
this folder.
